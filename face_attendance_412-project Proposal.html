Process

How It Works

Two phases — enrollment happens once. Attendance happens every class.

01

One-Time Student Enrollment

Done at the beginning of the semester. Never repeated again.

  • Student opens the app and logs in with their student ID
  • App guides them to scan their face from multiple angles
  • System saves a unique digital face profile in the cloud
  • Takes under 1 minute total
02

Taking Attendance (Every Class)

Fully automatic — teacher just takes one photo.

  • Teacher opens app, selects the course and date
  • Points phone camera at the classroom, captures one photo
  • System automatically detects and identifies all visible faces
  • Attendance marked instantly — present or absent with confidence score
  • Teacher reviews and can manually fix any mistakes

Application Modules

All Features

Seven core modules that cover the full attendance workflow — from enrollment to reporting.

📷 Student Enrollment

One-time face scan per student. Captures multiple angles to build a robust face profile stored securely in the cloud. Never repeated after initial setup.

🎯 Attendance Scan

Teacher captures a single photo of the entire class. InsightFace processes all faces in one pass and auto-marks present and absent students instantly.

📋 Attendance Records

Full history browsable by student, course, or date. Every session is logged digitally — no lost papers, no messy sheets.

📊 Reports & Charts

Visual attendance percentage per student. Pie and bar charts for quick insights. Identify students at risk of falling below the attendance threshold.

✏️ Manual Override

Teacher can correct any wrong or missed detection after the scan. Changes are reflected in real time in the database.

🔐 Secure Login

JWT-based authentication for both teachers and students. Passwords hashed with bcrypt. Role-based access — teachers and students see different views.

Known Challenges

Problems & Solutions

Every real-world obstacle identified and handled — the system is designed to be robust from day one.

👥
Multiple faces in one photo

InsightFace processes all faces in a single pass — no separate scan per student needed, no matter how crowded the classroom.

💡
Lighting & angle variation

Multi-angle enrollment makes the face profile robust to different lighting conditions and camera angles encountered in real classrooms.

🙈
Partially hidden faces

Faces that can't be confidently identified are flagged as unrecognized. The teacher uses the manual override to correct these cases.

📏
Far-away students

The app recommends an optimal camera distance before the teacher captures the photo — ensuring all students are within recognizable range.

🌐
Server cold start on free tier

The app pings the Render.com server before class begins to wake it up, ensuring there's no delay when attendance is actually taken.

Design Philosophy

Built for the real world

🔁 Graceful Degradation

When automatic recognition fails, the system falls back to manual override — teachers always have the final say. No attendance is ever lost.

🛡️ Tamper-Proof Records

Digital records stored in PostgreSQL with JWT-protected API endpoints. Proxy attendance becomes impossible — the camera sees the truth.

⚡ Performance First

InsightFace runs a single inference pass for the whole class photo — not one per student. Fast enough for real-time classroom use.

💸 Zero Cost

Every component runs on a free tier. The system is fully deployable with no budget — ideal for university projects and pilot programs.
